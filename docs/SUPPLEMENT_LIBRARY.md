# Forge supplement library

Forge provides **eight curated educational cards**, not a complete database, endorsement, diagnosis, or personal recommendation. The cards summarize evidence in broad terms and link to government and sports-science sources. They intentionally contain no dosage, stack, start/stop or interaction-screening instructions. Product labels and formulations differ; FDA does not pre-approve dietary supplements for safety or effectiveness before sale.

| Entry | Evidence framing | Main caution | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|

| Caffeine | May help some activities; response varies. | Sleep, anxiety, heart/blood-pressure effects, concentrated products and medicines. | [NIH ODS](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/), [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) |
| Beta-alanine | Mixed evidence for selected high-intensity contexts. | Temporary tingling; long-term safety evidence is limited. | [NIH ODS](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/), [AIS](https://www.ais.gov.au/nutrition/supplements/group_a) |
| Omega-3/fish oil | Exercise evidence remains mixed and outcome-specific. | Bleeding medicines/disorders, fish allergy, heart-rhythm context and procedures. | [NIH ODS](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Omega3FattyAcids-HealthProfessional/), [NCCIH](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/omega3-supplements-what-you-need-to-know), [AIS](https://www.ais.gov.au/nutrition/supplements/group_b) |

| Electrolyte products | Potentially useful in some prolonged/hot/sweaty activity; not automatically needed. | Too much fluid is dangerous; sodium/potassium and medical context matter. | [NIH ODS](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/), [AIS](https://www.ais.gov.au/nutrition/supplements/group_a), [NIH ODS potassium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Potassium-HealthProfessional/) |
| Iron | Treating confirmed iron-deficiency anemia may matter; not a general performance booster. | Toxic overdose, medicine interactions and iron-overload conditions; keep away from children. | [NIH ODS](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-HealthProfessional/), [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/small-entity-compliance-guide-label-warning-statements-iron-containing-supplements-and-drugs) |

A user-entered supplement reminder stores only its label and time; Forge does not choose a product or dose. Ask a clinician or pharmacist for individualized advice, especially for minors, pregnancy/breastfeeding, conditions, allergies, medicines, planned procedures or previous side effects. Do not change prescribed treatment based on an app card. Bring the complete product label and medication/supplement list for professional review.

The card set is non-exhaustive. Missing from the catalog does not mean a product is safe or unsafe. Safety summaries can be updated as authoritative guidance changes; the app records source URLs in the source module.
